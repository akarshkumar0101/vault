# Longterm Goals

What I want most from life, on a horizon of decades.

**In one line: become a high-status researcher who researches cool stuff.**

These feed my yearly goals (e.g. [[2027 Goals]]), which feed each monthly block (`system/YYYY-MM.md`), then `TODO.md`, then each day.

## 1. Understand the universe, and every universe that could be
I want a grand catalog of what is and what *could* be, across our physical universe, computational universes, and synthetic universes. For each topic below, the question is "how is it?" and also "how could it be?"
- **Physics:** how the universe works, how it came to be the way it is, and what other universes and laws are possible.
- **Life:** how life works, whether there are aliens out there, and the full space of possible life ("life as it could be").
- **Intelligence:** what intelligence is, how it works, and the full space of possible minds. Shedding light on that space is the core goal of artificial life research.
- **Emergence and open-endedness:** why things seem emergent, and why the universe keeps producing new complexity instead of plateauing.

## 2. Speed up civilization with AI
Help humanity by building very good, very capable AI systems that speed up its technological progress.

## 3. Status and freedom
Have enough status and career leverage in my field to do whatever I want, whenever I want, without asking anyone's permission:
- full control over which research directions I pursue, and when
- the option to start my own company at any time
- **The ambitious version:** win a Nobel Prize or Turing Award for my role in goals 1 and 2.
- **Become a professor later in life** (around age 35–40): my own lab, students, and the academic freedom to choose what to study. Keep academic ties alive along the way (publishing, collaborations) so this stays open.

## 4. Extreme financial security
Never have to worry about money again:
- take care of my mom, my dad, my brother, and my future family
- this means a net worth in the tens to hundreds of millions of dollars

## 5. Great health
Be a genuinely healthy person, in body and mind:
- **Body:** eat well, sleep well, train well, and take care of my body (injury prevention, longevity).
- **Discipline:** be someone who does what they planned to do.
- **Mind:** be healthy mentally, emotionally, and socially: strong relationships, real rest, no burnout.

## How they fit together
- **1 and 2 are the mission.** AI that speeds up science is also my best tool for understanding the universe, and my research on emergence and open-endedness sits where the two meet.
- **3 and 4 are the means.** Status and money buy the freedom to pursue 1 and 2 on my own terms and to protect my family. A Nobel or Turing Award would be the peak of 3, recognition for doing 1 and 2 at the highest level.
- **5 is the foundation.** Without health and discipline, none of the others last over decades. Each block's habit goals (sleep, food, training, recovery) serve this one directly.

## Using these goals
- **Big decisions:** weigh each option against all five goals. For example, the post-PhD choice: frontier lab vs. Recursive AI vs. Berkeley postdoc.
- **Revisit** these goals at least once a year, or whenever something big changes. Update the `updated` date when they change.

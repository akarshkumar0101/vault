## Habit goals
**Serves:** [[Longterm Goals]] #5 (great health).

1. **Sleep well:** 8–9 hours of restful sleep every night.
2. **Eat well:** eat healthy, and plan meals ahead of time (see [Food](#food)).
3. **Train well:** go to the gym and follow my gym plan. Play basketball **only when it's scheduled ahead of time**, so I don't overdo it and get injured.
4. **Work intentionally:** write TODOs *before* starting work, so I'm working toward concrete goals instead of mindlessly putting in hours.
5. **Work enough:** target **3–4 hours of deep work** and **1–2 hours of shallow/admin work** every day.
6. **Stop when work is done:** relax, rest, and recover fully, or I'll burn out.
